Source & Copyright Manager

Multi-platform

Collect candidate clips from YouTube, TikTok, Twitch and the wider web even when rights are not yet known. Save the original link and review each clip at the end. Only import media files that the source permits you to download or that you upload yourself; Auto AI will not bypass platform restrictions.

Search opens the platform's own results. Add any promising result below, even if its rights are unknown. These links are saved as candidates; they are not downloaded automatically.

Choose a saved script and press Find & collect clips. Auto AI will search the connected YouTube channel's permitted Creative Commons results and save matching candidates automatically.

Automatic discovery can collect source details and authorised previews. It cannot download YouTube/TikTok audiovisual files through their standard APIs. TikTok's Display API lists videos from an authorised creator account; Twitch download links require the relevant channel/editor authorisation.

Final review rule: rights status and your review decision are separate. You can save anything as a candidate, but a clip must be explicitly marked Approved with a review note before the YouTube upload flow will accept it. Approval is your own decision, not a legal clearance or a guarantee that reuse is lawful.
