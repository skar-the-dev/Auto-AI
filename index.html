How Auto AI works

The workspace is designed to keep creators in control of the content they approve and the accounts they connect.

Create a channel workspace. Set a channel name, content niche and preferred publishing interval.
Plan a batch. Select a channel, choose a niche, enter a topic or request a topic suggestion, and prepare script drafts.
Review sources and scripts. Check factual claims, source links, usage rights and draft wording before production.
Connect a destination when available. Authorise a supported social account using that platform's own sign-in and permission screen.
Review before publishing. Check the media, title, description, visibility and schedule, and explicitly authorise any upload.

Respect for creators and content rights

Finding a clip does not grant a licence to use it.
Auto AI stores source links and review notes so users can assess permissions. Users must only upload or publish media they own, have licensed, or are otherwise legally permitted to use. Auto AI does not bypass platform restrictions or remove creator attribution or watermarks.

Search results and previews are for discovery and review. Availability of downloads, imports, rendering and publishing depends on the source platform, account permissions, technical configuration and applicable approvals.
