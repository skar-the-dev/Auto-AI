Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

In brief

Auto AI uses information you provide to operate your content dashboard, save your accounts and scripts, and—if you choose—connect supported social-media accounts. OAuth tokens and administrator credentials are intended to be kept on the service side and protected. We do not sell personal information.

1. Who is responsible

Auto AI is the service described at auto-ai-c8y.pages.dev. For privacy questions or requests, contact aiauto.admin@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account and workspace details: names you give your Auto AI channels, content niches, selected platforms, publishing intervals and active/inactive settings.
  • Content and project data: topics, prompts, scripts, captions, source links, creator names, rights notes, review decisions and files you choose to upload.
  • Connected platform information: when you authorise a connection, information returned by that platform, such as a YouTube channel ID and title or a TikTok user ID, display name and avatar.
  • Authorisation data: OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain a connection. These are intended to be encrypted before storage and are not displayed as ordinary account details.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: information provided by your browser or hosting platform when requests are made, such as request metadata, timestamps and error information.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, special-category personal data or other sensitive information unless a feature explicitly requires it and you have a lawful reason to provide it.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain Auto AI; save and display your workspaces and scripts; perform the actions you request; connect to third-party platforms; protect the service; troubleshoot errors; and respond to support, privacy or legal requests.

4. Social-media connections

If you choose to connect YouTube or TikTok, you are redirected to that platform to sign in and grant permission. Auto AI receives only the information and permissions allowed by the authorisation you approve. You can disconnect a platform through the relevant account controls where available. Disconnecting stops future use of that stored connection; it does not necessarily delete information already held by the platform.

YouTube and TikTok process information under their own privacy policies. Review Google's Privacy Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the infrastructure and service providers needed to operate Auto AI, including Cloudflare hosting and database services, and by Google/YouTube or TikTok when you choose to connect those services. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect rights and security, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

6. Storage and security

Auto AI uses Cloudflare services for hosting and database storage. Social-platform tokens are designed to be encrypted before being stored. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your administrator key and devices secure, use strong unique passwords with your third-party accounts, and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain workspace, script, source and connection information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security and meet legal obligations. You may disconnect social accounts using available controls. To request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, email us from an address you control and describe the relevant Auto AI account. We may need to verify your request. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and to request data portability. If UK data protection law applies, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Contact us first and we will consider your request in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children

Auto AI is not designed for children and is not intended for use by anyone who cannot legally consent to the processing of their information under applicable law.

10. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers or connected platforms may process information in countries outside your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Auto AI changes. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email aiauto.admin@gmail.com.
